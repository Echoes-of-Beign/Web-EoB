The Journey Into Darkness

Emotions Vol. 1 – The Descent is a brutal reflection of the inner struggle. A sonic journey exploring solitude, shame, hate, and failure until reaching complete despair.

This album is not a simple collection of songs; it is a conceptual narrative that traces the emotional descent into hopelessness, combining profound lyrics, cinematic atmospheres, and overwhelming instrumental force.

Each track represents a negative emotion that drags the protagonist into a progressive process of internal degradation.

Act I: The Awakening

Cracks appear in emotional stability

Act II: The Conflict

The mind enters war with itself

Act III: The Fall

The emotional structure collapses

About Echoes of Beign

Echoes of Beign is a musical and artistic project conceived as a deep exploration of human emotions, consciousness, and the duality between light and darkness.

Combining alternative metal, industrial, and atmospheric elements with a conceptual narrative that treats each emotion as a living entity that possesses and transforms the human being.

This project is not a traditional band, but an artistic experience created through artificial intelligence, digital production, and human creative direction.

Through this fusion, it seeks to question the boundaries between organic and synthetic, emotional and artificial, proposing a new form of hybrid art where technology becomes an expressive instrument.
